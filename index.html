MY RESULTS

Your portraits, ready to revisit.

Open My Results at the bottom of the home screen to find your saved portraits. Previously unlocked results can be viewed again without another ad. Locked results stay obscured and lead back to the reveal screen. In-app result alerts also take you to this list.

History is stored on your device. Clear All asks for confirmation, then deletes the history, source-photo copies, thumbnails, and result images held by the app. Your gallery photos and images you have already exported or shared stay untouched.

PREMIUM & DAILY LIMITS

Create more versions of yourself.

Both free and Premium use have daily portrait allowances. Optional weekly, monthly, and yearly Premium subscriptions let you reveal completed portraits without rewarded ads. The app shows the included benefits and current allowance; Google Play shows the price and renewal terms before you confirm a purchase. Manage your subscription from Settings, and use Restore Purchases on the Premium screen when needed.

Ads depend on availability and consent requirements. If an ad cannot be completed, a free result stays locked so you can return and try again from My Results.
