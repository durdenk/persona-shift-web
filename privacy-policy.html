Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cukka Creatives handles information when you use PersonaShift (the app). PersonaShift does not require an account.

1. Information we process

  • Photos and generated results: The photo you choose or take, the style you select, and the generated portrait needed to provide the service.
  • Request data: Selected language, country code, app version, an app-specific installation identifier, premium state, selected style, job identifier, result-viewing/ad status, and ordinary connection data such as IP address.
  • Feedback: When you use Settings > Contact us, your message, app name, app version, and the same app-specific installation identifier used for portrait requests are sent to our feedback service. No name or email field is required. Your message can include personal information if you choose to provide it.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location inferred from network signals, consent choices, diagnostics, and ad-interaction data when its SDK initializes or requests and serves ads. This processing is not limited to the moment you finish watching an ad.
  • Purchase data: Google Play supplies purchase state, entitlement, product and base-plan identifiers, and a purchase token needed to process or restore purchases. We do not receive complete payment-card details.
  • Analytics, diagnostics, and configuration: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics process app interactions, portrait-creation and purchase events, errors, and device/app information. Firebase Remote Config uses installation identifiers to deliver app configuration, including daily portrait allowances. These services may operate for both free and Premium users.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: Used only when you choose to take a photo in the app.
  • System photo picker: Used when you choose a photo. The app does not request broad access to your photo library for this action.
  • Internet: Used to send portrait-creation requests, deliver results, load rewarded ads, process purchases, and provide diagnostics.
  • Vibration: Used for brief tactile feedback after interactions.

3. How photos and feedback are used

When you create a portrait, the selected photo and its request metadata are sent to our backend and image-processing providers so the result can be created and delivered. We do not sell your photos or use them for unrelated advertising. Photos and results are processed for request handling, result delivery, abuse prevention, and service operation.

Feedback is submitted only when you press Send in the Contact us form. We use the message and its technical context to review support requests, improve the app, and manage abuse or duplicate submissions. The form does not attach your portrait or generated result.

4. Ads and purchases

For free users, unlocking a completed portrait requires completing a rewarded ad. Active Premium users can reveal results without this ad step. Previously unlocked portraits can be reopened from My Results without another ad. Ad availability is not guaranteed; an unsuccessful or unavailable ad does not unlock a free result.

Google AdMob may process ad requests, impressions, clicks, consent choices, and earned rewards. The app uses Google's consent tools before requesting ads, and Settings shows Privacy choices when the consent service requires that option. Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. Current prices, billing periods, renewal terms, cancellations, and refunds are managed through Google Play.

5. Storage and sharing

The app stores preferences, portrait history, unlock state, source-photo copies, thumbnails, downloaded result images, job identifiers, daily usage counters, and premium state in private app storage. History is stored on the device; the app does not provide account-based history synchronization and disables Android backup/device transfer of its app data.

If you save an image to your gallery or share it through Android's share sheet, that copy is outside the app's private history storage. Your selected recipient or receiving app handles shared copies under its own policies. We also use service providers for image processing, result delivery, feedback, advertising, purchases, analytics, diagnostics, configuration, security, and legal compliance.

6. Retention and deletion

  • In-app history: Open My Results from the home screen, select Clear All, and confirm. This removes history records, source-photo copies, thumbnails, downloaded results, and cached previews held by the app. It does not delete your original gallery photos, exported or shared copies, preferences, daily usage counters, or your subscription.
  • Other local app data: Clear the app's data in Android Settings or uninstall the app to remove its private data and preferences. Gallery exports and copies held by recipients remain outside that cleanup.
  • Server-side data: Local cleanup does not send a deletion request to the backend and does not remove previously submitted feedback. Server-side photos, request metadata, results, and feedback are retained according to our backend and service providers' operational retention rules. To request deletion, email cukkacreatives@gmail.com with the subject “PersonaShift data deletion request” and include the approximate date, time, and style or feedback submission involved. Do not send another copy of your photo unless needed to identify the request.

7. Children’s privacy

PersonaShift is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Entertainment notice

Generated images are synthetic entertainment content and may be inaccurate or unrealistic. They do not establish a person’s identity, age, background, or other real-world characteristics.

9. Contact and changes

We may update this policy and publish the revised version on this page. For questions, contact cukkacreatives@gmail.com.
