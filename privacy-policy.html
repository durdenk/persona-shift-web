Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cukka Creatives handles information when you use PersonaShift (the app). PersonaShift does not require an account.

1. Information we process

  • Photos and generated results: The photo you choose or take, the style you select, and the generated image needed to provide the transformation.
  • Request data: Selected language, country code, app version, an app-specific installation identifier, premium state, selected style, job identifier, and ordinary connection data such as IP address.
  • Advertising data: Advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location inferred from network signals, consent choices, and ad-interaction data may be processed by Google AdMob when an ad is shown.
  • Purchase data: Google Play supplies purchase state, entitlement, product identifiers, and a purchase token needed to process or restore purchases. We do not receive complete payment-card details.
  • Diagnostics: App interactions, transformation events, purchase events, ad outcomes, errors, and device/app information may be collected through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: Used only when you choose to take a photo in the app.
  • System photo picker: Used when you choose a photo. The app does not request broad access to your photo library for this action.
  • Internet: Used to send transformation requests, deliver results, load rewarded ads, process purchases, and provide diagnostics.
  • Vibration: Used for brief tactile feedback after interactions.

3. How photos are used

When you begin a transformation, the selected photo and the request data listed above are sent to our backend and image-processing providers so the result can be created and delivered. We do not sell your photos or use them for unrelated advertising. Submitted photos and results may be retained temporarily to process requests, deliver results, prevent abuse, and operate the service.

4. Ads and purchases

The app may offer a voluntary rewarded ad to reveal a result. Google AdMob may process ad requests, impressions, clicks, consent choices, and earned rewards. Optional Premium subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing. Current prices, billing periods, renewal terms, cancellations, and refunds are managed through Google Play.

5. Storage and sharing

The app stores preferences, transformation history, thumbnails, downloaded images, job identifiers, daily usage counters, and premium state in private app storage. This data is removed when you clear app data or uninstall the app. We share data only with service providers needed for image processing, result delivery, advertising, purchases, diagnostics, security, and legal compliance.

6. Retention and deletion

Server-side photos, request metadata, and results are retained according to the operational retention period of our backend and service providers. To request deletion of server-side data, email cukkacreatives@gmail.com with the subject “PersonaShift data deletion request” and include the approximate date, time, and style used.

7. Children’s privacy

PersonaShift is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Entertainment notice

Generated images are synthetic entertainment content and may be inaccurate or unrealistic. They do not establish a person’s identity, age, background, or other real-world characteristics.

9. Contact and changes

We may update this policy and publish the revised version on this page. For questions, contact cukkacreatives@gmail.com.
