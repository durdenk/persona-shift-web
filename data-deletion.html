Delete your PersonaShift data

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This page explains how to delete data related to PersonaShift, the Android app by Cukka Creatives. PersonaShift has no user accounts, so there is no account to delete: your portrait history lives on your phone, and only the data needed to create portraits and run the app leaves it. You can delete most of it yourself, and you can ask us to delete the rest.

1. Delete the data on your phone

Your portrait history, source-photo copies, thumbnails, generated results, unlock state, daily usage counters, settings and Premium status are saved only in the app's private storage on your device.

  • Portrait history only: open My Results from the home screen, tap Clear All and confirm. This removes history records, source-photo copies, thumbnails and result images held by the app.
  • Everything on your phone: open your phone's Settings → Apps → PersonaShift → Storage and tap Clear storage (or Clear data), or uninstall PersonaShift.

This cannot be undone. PersonaShift turns off Android backup and device transfer for its data, so no copy stays in your Google account backup. Images you saved to your gallery or shared with others are outside the app and are not removed. Deleting data or uninstalling the app does not cancel a Premium subscription; manage it in Google Play.

2. Ask us to delete your data

To ask us to delete data held on our servers:

  1. Email cukkacreatives@gmail.com with the subject "PersonaShift data deletion request".
  2. Because there are no accounts, tell us how to find your data: the approximate date and time, and the style you used, of the portraits, the feedback messages or the reports you want deleted. If you emailed us before, send your request from the same address.
  3. Do not send another copy of your photo unless we ask for it to find your request. Do not send payment card details, passwords or other sensitive information.

We reply to confirm your request and delete the data within 30 days.

3. What is deleted

  • Photos and generated portraits you sent for processing, with their request data (style, language, country code, app version and installation identifier). Our server also deletes older portrait jobs automatically, without a request.
  • Feedback messages you sent from Settings → Contact us.
  • Content reports you sent with the Report button on a result screen, including the copy of the reported result, unless we must keep them as described below.
  • Analytics and crash data held by Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics) on our behalf. It is tied to random app identifiers that we cannot link to you by name or email. Where we can identify your data, we delete it; otherwise it expires under Firebase's data retention rules.
  • Emails you sent us, including your deletion request, once the request is handled.

4. What is kept

  • Content reports about possibly illegal or harmful content may be kept as long as needed to review them, keep the service safe and meet legal obligations.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play as long as tax, accounting and fraud-prevention laws require. We do not store them on a server of our own.

5. Data controlled by Google

Advertising data collected through Google AdMob, Google Play reviews and Google Play purchase records are controlled by Google. You can manage them yourself:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path depends on your phone).
  • Change your ad consent choices with Privacy choices in the app's Settings, where it is shown.
  • Cancel a Premium subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or from Settings → Manage subscription in the app.
  • Edit or delete your review on the PersonaShift page in Google Play.
  • Manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.

More details are in our Privacy Policy.
